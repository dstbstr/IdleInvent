Ideal Idual Iduel Idle

Two modes: vs and cooperative
Timebox: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month
Complexity: Simple, Standard, Complex


In vs mode
	Played against an AI
	AI either plays optimially or through some AI training
	AI effects are negative (1.0 - 0.5, purchase delay + 0.5, time limit - 10 seconds)
In Cooperative mode
	Work with the AI to achieve the maximum score
	Player and AI both earn resources from generator, but have separate wallets
	AI effects are positive (1.0 + 0.5, purchase delay - 0.5, time limit + 10 seconds)

Player Bonuses
	Score multiplier
	Extra time
	Resource head start
	Resource multiplier
	View AI's next purchase
	ETA to purchase
	Early termination (if player's score is higher than AI's final score, or if player + AI meets target score)
	Eventual score
	Taps per tap
AI Effects
	Score modifier
	Purchase delay
	Time limit
	Taps per second
