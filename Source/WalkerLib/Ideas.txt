The journey there and back again
Travel to a location, pick up the MacGuffin, return it for profit.
Vehicles: Foot, bike, car, boat, plane, jet, rocket, starship
Jobs: Scientist, engineer, navigator
Destinations: Across Town, the city, the next state, Hawaii, Europe, the Moon, Mars, Pluto, Alpha Centauri, Andromeda Galaxy, Virgo Supercluster, edge of the universe, beyond
Attributes: Max speed, max acceleration, cargo+crew capacity, Load/Unload speed, fuel capacity, fuel efficiency
Rebirth: (Rebirth) Multipliers to attributes, (Prestige) exponents to multipliers, (Ascend) ??
Pop-up Events: Distress call (add crew), Shortcut (reduce journey distance), Fuel leak (double fuel consumption)

CargoSpace is shared between Cargo, Crew, and Fuel. Fuel is spent during the journey
